# Ghumne V1 business policies

Recorded: 2026-10-20. Authority: the owner's provisional V1 instructions. These policies guide the review draft; they are not final commercial terms, legal advice, a deployed policy or authorization to execute migrations.

## Owner-directed provisional policies

| Area                 | V1 policy                                                                                         | Database representation                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Registration         | Agencies register for free. Registration does not approve the company or grant publishing rights. | `register_agency` creates pending company, one owner membership and agency capability; no subscription charge or automatic paid plan.                                                                                       |
| Verification         | Business verification and approval are manual.                                                    | Private verification metadata, reviewer/time/reason, immutable approval history. No invented company or verification evidence.                                                                                              |
| Moderation           | New packages and material changes require moderation.                                             | Draft -> pending_review -> approved/rejected revisions; immutable approved content and child records; a separate publication transition.                                                                                    |
| Freshness            | Packages must be reconfirmed every 30 days.                                                       | Default setting 30 days; published revision ID and confirmation time must match. At the boundary the package loses public eligibility until reconfirmed. Availability has its own confirmation time.                        |
| Pricing              | NPR, per-person and group totals.                                                                 | Integer minor units (100 per rupee), explicit group tiers/units, mandatory fees and incomplete-quote states.                                                                                                                |
| Conversion           | Inquiries, WhatsApp and external agency booking links.                                            | Protected inquiry inbox and validated contact/booking fields; no payment, reservation or commission tables.                                                                                                                 |
| Subscriptions        | Free pilot, monthly and annual models; configurable prices and entitlements.                      | Immutable plan versions and manually assigned periods. Only the free-pilot price is necessarily zero; no actual commercial plan records are seeded.                                                                         |
| Expiry               | Expired subscriptions do not delete company data.                                                 | Eligibility is time-bound; agency drafts, packages, subscription history and inquiries remain stored and tenant-readable.                                                                                                   |
| Promotion            | Sponsored placements must be labeled.                                                             | Label constrained to `Sponsored`, separate placement records/projection, current entitlement checked when served.                                                                                                           |
| Organic ranking      | Prioritize traveler relevance.                                                                    | Entitlement only controls eligibility/allowance; it is not an organic relevance multiplier. Full Finder ranking remains a later application task.                                                                           |
| Privacy              | Collect minimal personal data and make retention configurable.                                    | Auth owns account credentials; profiles have display/avatar only; inquiry requires name plus email or phone and consent version. No contact/message payload in event/audit metadata. Retention categories are configurable. |
| Persistence/security | Supabase PostgreSQL and RLS.                                                                      | Explicit table grants, RLS, trusted grants/memberships, narrow server-authorized workflows and curated public RPCs.                                                                                                         |

## Engineering assumptions for review

- One active owner per agency initially. Staff access and ownership transfer need a separately reviewed workflow. Agency membership does not prevent personal traveler use.
- Subscription assignment/renewal is manual. Monthly and annual periods mean one Nepal calendar month/year from the selected start, stored as UTC instants with exclusive end times. Pilot duration is explicitly chosen by admin. No automatic renewal, charge, proration or invoice is implied.
- No implicit grace period. An expired/suspended subscription or agency immediately fails public eligibility. An over-limit downgrade requires pausing excess published listings first; no arbitrary record deletion. Retiring a plan also removes eligibility for its assigned versions, so use a new version for ordinary price changes and review retirement effects carefully.
- Listing quota counts published packages, including stale or temporarily ineligible published records; draft revisions do not consume extra slots. Package publication and subscription transitions serialize on the agency row.
- Reconfirmation acknowledges the currently approved package terms. It does not permit editing the itinerary/price in place or assert that seats are reserved. Date availability must be separately refreshed; unknown capacity/dates cannot produce an exact available match.
- V1 uses a uniform traveler rate, fixed duration, reviewed person/group base tiers and person/group fees per trip/day/night. The owner explicitly confirmed discounted group tiers plus rejection of negative fees and unsupported discount types for this preparation. Coupons, percentage/stacking discounts and child/age/room-specific rates require an explicit later model. Unknown necessary exclusions prevent claiming whole-trip affordability.
- All new approved revisions need current pricing, a primary destination, origin, interest tags, a complete day sequence and source notes. A reviewed revision does not publish itself.
- Sponsored allocation is manually administered. Overlap accounting is conservative; serving also checks current allowance, package visibility and placement dates. Promotion never purchases an exact match or organic priority.
- The trusted future inquiry endpoint must validate user identity/contact/consent and origin/CSRF, derive a secret HMAC abuse key, and call the backend-only RPC. The SQL default is ten successful submissions per key/hour, configurable; retries with the same request/payload return the existing result. This is a technical abuse-control assumption, not a commercial limit.
- Inquiry collection defaults **off**. New collection needs an explicit enablement and a configured inquiry retention duration/note. Retention durations otherwise start unconfigured and purging disabled; no deletion worker is installed. Enable collection only after retention execution, consent and server controls are reviewed. Existing idempotent retries do not collect another record.
- Private verification uploads are not enabled by this schema. Only references/metadata are prepared; actual Storage buckets/policies, upload validation and evidence handling need a separate implementation/review.
- SQL RPCs are foundation contracts. No marketplace UI, authenticated route, privileged server client or messaging integration is enabled by preparing them.

## Final commercial and operational decisions still open

1. Plan names, monthly/annual NPR prices, listing limits, analytics levels, pilot duration/eligibility, renewal administration and any grace terms.
2. Exact verification evidence, reviewer independence, appeals, re-verification/ownership transfer and moderation turnaround.
3. Sponsored allocation, surface limits, scheduling/commercial terms and what happens to future placements after plan changes.
4. Retention periods/triggers for inquiries, verification, events, audit, abuse keys, operation keys, profiles and trips; final consent wording, account closure and deletion/archival procedure. Record expiry alone never deletes agency data.
5. Whether later versions need child rates, occupancy pricing, flexible durations, currencies or discounts.
6. Approved test/production targets, credentials, first-admin identity, Storage policy, hosting and release approvals. No paid provider is selected.

Do not infer agreement to these final decisions from silence. V1 assumptions are documented so they can be reviewed and changed before database application or public launch.
